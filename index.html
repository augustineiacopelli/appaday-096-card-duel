Card Duel

Two players, one device. Send out creatures, knock the other side down, then go for the stars.

How to play

Your turn: draw one card, send out one creature, then attack with any creature that is awake.

Attack: tap your creature, then tap what it hits. Both creatures deal their attack number to each other at the same time. At zero health, a creature is knocked out.

Stars: every creature you knock out costs its trainer one star. If the other side has no creatures at all, your attack takes a star straight from the trainer. First to take all six stars wins.

New creatures nap the turn they arrive. They wake up on your next turn.
